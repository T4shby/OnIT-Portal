# Customer requester SSO

Canonical model: **SuperOps Client SSO per customer tenant**.

See [SuperOpsRequesterSsoSetup.md](SuperOpsRequesterSsoSetup.md) for the full runbook.

## Architecture

Each customer has:

- One SuperOps Client SSO configuration.
- One non-gallery Entra SAML enterprise app in the customer's own tenant.
- A client-specific Entity ID and Consumer Service URL generated by SuperOps.
- The customer's Azure Login URL and certificate stored in that SuperOps configuration.
- The same lowercase `email`, `firstname`, `lastname` claims.

Customer users remain Members of their own Entra tenant. Client SSO does not create B2B guest accounts in On IT.

## Access

- P1: assign `On IT Portal - {Company}` to the customer Client SSO enterprise app.
- Free: save the Client SSO Application (client) ID on the portal; Sync now assigns active licensed users directly.
- Requesters must also exist in SuperOps through the separate SCIM app with the same email.

## Retired model

Do not use the On IT Global requester SSO app for customer tenants. The former Multitenant + per-customer admin-consent experiment is retired.

Technician SSO remains a separate On IT application.
